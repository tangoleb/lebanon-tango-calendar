# lebanon-tango-calendar
Lebanon Argentine Tango Calendar

Subscribe to this Calendar: You can sync Lebanon's Argentine Tango Events directly to your phone, tablet, or computer. Simply copy the public .ics link below and paste it into your preferred calendar application (such as Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Microsoft Outlook, etc) using their "Add Calendar from URL" or "Subscribe" option. Once added, your device will automatically pull updates in the background whenever new events are added or changed here, keeping your schedule perfectly up to date without any manual downloads.
